Story

Our hero "Naylor" is on an adventure known as "The Lad Alarm Train Crawl".

Throughout the day he will be arriving at train stations and visiting local pubs. Between pubs he will be on a train before a short journey to the next local pub.

Starting point will be Dorchester train station and "Brewhouse". 

Train stops and pubs.

- Dorchester, Brewhouse 
- Wool, The Black Bear (angry locals)
- Wareham, The Old Grainary (an art easel ends up in a local river)
- Parkstone, The Avocet


Between Wareham and Parkstone, Naylor comes across his arch nemesis "The Train Conductor". This will be a boss battle of the level and needs our hero to defeat him. 

Location: Southern England, UK. The train should appear like a British train and pubs should all look like traidtional pubs. Wareham is by a river. 

The pubs can appear in the background as buildings. Naylor should be hopping in a train like objects as the platforms. 


Our hero should be collecting 'pints' glasses of a stout style looking drinking. Black body with a white top. 

There should also be a bonus espresso martini item hidden in the level.
